# Why items are missing from the index — measured, 2 September 2026

Written after the owner reported that they and Shara searched for several items and
some did not appear. Everything below is a count I produced against the live wiki and
this repository's own payload; the command that produced each is named.

**The short answer: there are two separate causes, and the larger one is a policy this
project chose on purpose.**

| | count |
|---|---|
| Items we ship | **3,663** |
| Items we hold back | **7,599** |
| — of those, held back as out-of-era (Kunark, Velious, Luclin, Epic Quests…) | 5,343 |
| — of those, held back as **"no era in any source"** | **2,230** (447 wearable) |
| Live wiki `Category:Items` pages, 2 Sep | **11,168** |
| Our vendored wiki scrape (`eqlwiki-items-2026-08-03.json`) | 6,903 |
| Live titles absent from that scrape | 4,410 |
| **Live titles this project has never seen in any form** | **207** |

`node pipeline/probe-wiki-gap.mjs` produces the last four rows.

---

## 1. The catalogue is a month stale, and the wiki is being edited today

The vendored scrape is dated **3 August 2026**. It is now **2 September**. The wiki's
`eqlmetadata` API reports `Earthshaker` was `touched` at `20260902090514` — an hour
before this was written.

More importantly the wiki has grown structure we have never read. Its page HTML carries
a `RLCONF` block declaring custom EQL MediaWiki extensions:

```
wgEQLMetadataApiAction : "eqlmetadata"      a custom API action (POST-only)
wgEQLEraConfigRevision : 156232
wgEQLEraOutKeys        : kunark, velious, luclin, chardok, chardokrevamp, holevp,
                         warrensfearhaterevamp, fearhaterevamp, epics, epicquests, unknown
wgEQLVerifiedPagesTitle: "VerifiedPages"     a community verification registry
wgEQLVerifiedPagesRevision: 175780
wgEQLPageVerified      : false               per-page verification flag
```

`wgEQLEraOutKeys` is the wiki's own out-of-era list and it matches this project's
`ERA_ORDER` exclusions closely. **The wiki now models era and verification as
first-class data.** Our pipeline models neither from this source.

## 2. `VerifiedPages` — a provenance signal we are not using

`https://eqlwiki.com/api.php?action=parse&page=VerifiedPages&prop=wikitext` returns a
**1,167-entry** list of pages the wiki community has verified.

| cross-reference | count |
|---|---|
| Shipped items that are wiki-**verified** | **394** of 3,663 |
| Era-less quarantined items that are verified | **84** of 2,230 |
| — of the 447 wearable era-less | **25** |
| Out-of-era quarantined that are verified | 24 of 5,343 |

Under `research/SOURCING-STANDARD.md` a community verification is not an observation,
but it is a stronger claim than an unverified scrape. **394 shipped items could carry a
better-evidenced standing than they do now**, and 84 items we withhold have been checked
by someone.

## 3. The 207 we have never seen

Of the 207 live titles absent from the payload *and* the quarantine:

- **74** declare a `Slot:` — wearable gear
- **41** of those carry real stats
- **169** carry a `{{Classic Era}}` template
- **38** are wearable **and** Classic **and** statted — i.e. **they would ship today
  under the existing era policy, with no policy change at all.**

Among the 38: the whole **Anthemion** set (Jerkin, Gloves, Boots, Skullcap, Leggings),
**Ancient Spurned** Ferraiolo and Waistcoat, `Gilded Mistmoore Visor`,
`Visage of the Gargoyle`, `Zraxthril Forged Flamberge`, `Cherista's Fangs`,
`Shimmering Bracer of Protection` — and **`Shadow Rage Tunic`**, which is the same set as
the `Shadow Rage Helm` this repository's own Tier 0 character wears and cannot score.

## 4. A hypothesis I tested and had to withdraw

The 207 mostly carry `{{Classic Era}}` templates, and this project's era derivation reads
five explicit fields — `eqlwiki.available_from`, `eqlwiki.era`, `eqlwiki.eras.min`,
`jmoyers.eraTag`, `nathanbates.era` — **and never the era template.** So the obvious
hypothesis was that the 2,230 era-less records are a parsing miss.

Measured against the live wiki for all 447 wearable era-less records:

```
{{X Era}} template found on the page:
   419  (none)
    28  Classic
```

**The hypothesis is wrong.** Only 28 are recoverable that way. For the other 419 the wiki
does not state an era either — they are genuinely unplaced, not mis-parsed. The era gate
is doing what it was designed to do.

A second idea also under-delivered: item pages record `dropsfrom`, and five of our eight
eras *are* zones. But only **59 of 447** carry a `dropsfrom` block, dominated by
Plane of Hate (37) and Plane of Fear (23) — both in-era. Useful, and overlapping with the
28 above, but not a general solution.

## 5. What can be fixed with no policy decision

**66 wearable items**, each with wiki evidence:

- **38** never-seen, wearable, Classic-era, statted (§3)
- **28** currently quarantined era-less, which the live wiki now tags Classic (§4)

Every one of these ships under the era rule exactly as it stands. This is pure gap
closure and needs no ruling.

## 6. What needs the owner's decision

**~419 wearable items** (and ~1,780 non-wearable) are withheld because **no source states
an era**. `web/public/data/meta.json` records the current rule:

> *"Era-less is unconfirmed, not presumed classic: the few era-less items that do ship are
> the ones Tier 0 vouches for, and they carry `eraUnknown:true`."*

That rule is deliberate and defensible. It is also, on the evidence above, **the main
reason a player searches for an item and finds nothing.** The options are a decision, not
a bug fix:

1. Keep the rule. Searches keep missing ~2,230 records.
2. Ship era-less items **marked as unplaced**, the way `eraUnknown:true` already allows,
   so they are findable but never silently presented as confirmed in-era.
3. Ship only the era-less items that are on `VerifiedPages` (84 records, 25 wearable) —
   a middle path with an external check behind it.

My recommendation is **(2) restricted to wearable items**, because a gear planner that
cannot find a piece of gear is failing at its one job, and `eraUnknown` is a disclosure
device that already exists and is already rendered. But this changes what the site
asserts, so it is the owner's call and not mine.

## 7. Other EQL sites found, and what each is good for

Discovered by web search, then each verified by fetching it.

| site | live | what it is | usable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **eqlwiki.com** | ✅ MediaWiki 1.45.3 | the source we already vendor; now has era + verification extensions | **yes** — API open, already in use |
| **gnollguard.com** | ✅ | community item DB + loot-tracker desktop app; drop locations, vendor prices, quest rewards | **NO — see below** |
| **eqlbase.com** | ✅ | maps, item planner, tri-class calculator, **spell search** | robots allows all |
| **eqldb.org** | ✅ | character profiles; claims EQL Wiki data adapted **CC BY-SA 4.0** | worth reading on licensing |
| **eqlegendsdb.com** | ✅ | character profiles, build loadouts, live item data | robots allows all |
| **eqlegends.wiki** | ✅ | a second "EQL Wiki" — but its `robots.txt` points at `buildasoccersquadwiki.wiki` | treat with suspicion |

### gnollguard.com is off limits, and it is the one we would most want

Its `robots.txt` names AI agents explicitly and refuses them:

```
User-Agent: GPTBot
User-Agent: OAI-SearchBot
User-Agent: ChatGPT-User
User-Agent: ClaudeBot
User-Agent: Claude-Web
User-Agent: anthropic-ai
...
Disallow: /
```

The network egress proxy blocks it independently (`EGRESS_BLOCKED`). **I did not fetch
anything from it beyond `robots.txt`, and will not.** It is a crowd-sourced loot database
with drop locations — precisely the data that would close the gap in §6 with real
observations. If we want it, the route is a person asking its operator, not a crawler.

### On licensing

`eqldb.org` describes itself as using "EQL Wiki catalogue data adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0".
This project's own `meta.json` states the opposite about the upstream:

> *"eqlwiki publishes no content licence — checked 2026-08-18; siteinfo rightsinfo empty,
> Project:Copyrights absent."*

A third party's claim about someone else's licence is not authority, and I have **not**
re-checked eqlwiki's `rightsinfo` as part of this. If the wiki has since published a
licence that would be a material change to this project's attribution position and is
worth a deliberate check.

## 8. Spells: we do not model them at all

The shipped payload is `items-index.json`, `items/*.json`, `focus-effects.json`,
`contamination.json`, `meta.json`. **There is no spell data.** The app's only use of the
word is item spell-mods and focus effects, and `focus-effects.json` deliberately carries
"no numbers a planner could score".

The owner asked about **Call of Flame** for Rangers. The wiki has it, fully structured:

```
Ranger — Level 49 · 300 damage · 106 mana · 0.50s cast · 10.00s recast
Skill: Evocation · Resist: Fire (0) · from the Call of Flame Quest · {{Classic Era}}
```

`jmoyers-spells.json`, `jmoyers-class-spells.json` and `jmoyers-spelllines-merged.json`
are already vendored in `research/data/` and are read by nothing.

Adding spells is a **scope expansion**, not a gap fix — it is a second entity type, a
second ranking model, and a second payload. It should be a deliberate decision.

**And to answer the question directly: no other session has told me anything about spell
upgrades.** I have no working channel to sessions C, D or E — `ListAgents` reports no
reachable peers and cross-session messaging fails at the auth layer. Everything I know
about their work comes from the Director's `HANDOFF.md`.

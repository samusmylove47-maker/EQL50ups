# Why items are missing from the index — measured, 2 September 2026

Written after the owner reported that they and Shara searched for several items and
some did not appear. Everything below is a count I produced against the live wiki and
this repository's own payload; the command that produced each is named.

**The short answer: there are two separate causes, and the larger one is a policy this
project chose on purpose.**

| | count |
|---|---|
| Items we ship | **3,663** |
| Items we hold back | **7,599** |
| — of those, held back as out-of-era (Kunark, Velious, Luclin, Epic Quests…) | 5,343 |
| — of those, held back as **"no era in any source"** | **2,230** (447 wearable) |
| Live wiki `Category:Items` pages, 2 Sep | **11,168** |
| Our vendored wiki scrape (`eqlwiki-items-2026-08-03.json`) | 6,903 |
| Live titles absent from that scrape | 4,410 |
| **Live titles this project has never seen in any form** | **207** |

`node pipeline/probe-wiki-gap.mjs` produces the last four rows.

## 1. The catalogue is a month stale, and the wiki is being edited today

The vendored scrape is dated **3 August 2026**. It is now **2 September**. The wiki's
`eqlmetadata` API reports `Earthshaker` was `touched` at `20260902090514` — an hour
before this was written.

More importantly the wiki has grown structure we have never read. Its page HTML carries
a `RLCONF` block declaring custom EQL MediaWiki extensions:

```
wgEQLMetadataApiAction : "eqlmetadata"      a custom API action (POST-only)
wgEQLEraConfigRevision : 156232
wgEQLEraOutKeys        : kunark, velious, luclin, chardok, chardokrevamp, holevp,
                         warrensfearhaterevamp, fearhaterevamp, epics, epicquests, unknown
wgEQLVerifiedPagesTitle: "VerifiedPages"     a community verification registry
wgEQLVerifiedPagesRevision: 175780
wgEQLPageVerified      : false               per-page verification flag
```

`wgEQLEraOutKeys` is the wiki's own out-of-era list and it matches this project's
`ERA_ORDER` exclusions closely. **The wiki now models era and verification as
first-class data.** Our pipeline models neither from this source.

## 2. `VerifiedPages` — a provenance signal we are not using

`https://eqlwiki.com/api.php?action=parse&page=VerifiedPages&prop=wikitext` returns a
**1,167-entry** list of pages the wiki community has verified.

| cross-reference | count |
|---|---|
| Shipped items that are wiki-**verified** | **394** of 3,663 |
| Era-less quarantined items that are verified | **84** of 2,230 |
| — of the 447 wearable era-less | **25** |
| Out-of-era quarantined that are verified | 24 of 5,343 |

Under `research/SOURCING-STANDARD.md` a community verification is not an observation,
but it is a stronger claim than an unverified scrape. **394 shipped items could carry a
better-evidenced standing than they do now**, and 84 items we withhold have been checked
by someone.

## 3. The 207 we have never seen

Of the 207 live titles absent from the payload *and* the quarantine:

- **74** declare a `Slot:` — wearable gear
- **41** of those carry real stats
- **169** carry a `{{Classic Era}}` template
- **38** are wearable **and** Classic **and** statted — i.e. **they would ship today
  under the existing era policy, with no policy change at all.**

Among the 38: the whole **Anthemion** set (Jerkin, Gloves, Boots, Skullcap, Leggings),
**Ancient Spurned** Ferraiolo and Waistcoat, `Gilded Mistmoore Visor`,
`Visage of the Gargoyle`, `Zraxthril Forged Flamberge`, `Cherista's Fangs`,
`Shimmering Bracer of Protection` — and **`Shadow Rage Tunic`**, which is the same set as
the `Shadow Rage Helm` this repository's own Tier 0 character wears and cannot score.

## 4. A hypothesis I tested and had to withdraw

The 207 mostly carry `{{Classic Era}}` templates, and this project's era derivation reads
five explicit fields — `eqlwiki.available_from`, `eqlwiki.era`, `eqlwiki.eras.min`,
`jmoyers.eraTag`, `nathanbates.era` — **and never the era template.** So the obvious
hypothesis was that the 2,230 era-less records are a parsing miss.

Measured against the live wiki for all 447 wearable era-less records:

```
{{X Era}} template found on the page:
   419  (none)
    28  Classic
```

**The hypothesis is wrong.** Only 28 are recoverable that way. For the other 419 the wiki
does not state an era either — they are genuinely unplaced, not mis-parsed. The era gate
is doing what it was designed to do.

A second idea also under-delivered: item pages record `dropsfrom`, and five of our eight
eras *are* zones. But only **59 of 447** carry a `dropsfrom` block, dominated by
Plane of Hate (37) and Plane of Fear (23) — both in-era. Useful, and overlapping with the
28 above, but not a general solution.

## 5. What can be fixed with no policy decision

**66 wearable items**, each with wiki evidence:

- **38** never-seen, wearable, Classic-era, statted (§3)
- **28** currently quarantined era-less, which the live wiki now tags Classic (§4)

Every one of these ships under the era rule exactly as it stands. This is pure gap
closure and needs no ruling.

## 6. What needs the owner's decision

**~419 wearable items** (and ~1,780 non-wearable) are withheld because **no source states
an era**. `web/public/data/meta.json` records the current rule:

> *"Era-less is unconfirmed, not presumed classic: the few era-less items that do ship are
> the ones Tier 0 vouches for, and they carry `eraUnknown:true`."*

That rule is deliberate and defensible. It is also, on the evidence above, **the main
reason a player searches for an item and finds nothing.** The options are a decision, not
a bug fix:

1. Keep the rule. Searches keep missing ~2,230 records.
2. Ship era-less items **marked as unplaced**, the way `eraUnknown:true` already allows,
   so they are findable but never silently presented as confirmed in-era.
3. Ship only the era-less items that are on `VerifiedPages` (84 records, 25 wearable) —
   a middle path with an external check behind it.

My recommendation is **(2) restricted to wearable items**, because a gear planner that
cannot find a piece of gear is failing at its one job, and `eraUnknown` is a disclosure
device that already exists and is already rendered. But this changes what the site
asserts, so it is the owner's call and not mine.

## 7. Other EQL sites found, and what each is good for

Discovered by web search, then each verified by fetching it.

| site | live | what it is | usable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **eqlwiki.com** | ✅ MediaWiki 1.45.3 | the source we already vendor; now has era + verification extensions | **yes** — API open, already in use |
| **gnollguard.com** | ✅ | community item DB + loot-tracker desktop app; drop locations, vendor prices, quest rewards | **NO — see below** |
| **eqlbase.com** | ✅ | maps, item planner, tri-class calculator, **spell search** | robots allows all |
| **eqldb.org** | ✅ | character profiles; claims EQL Wiki data adapted **CC BY-SA 4.0** | worth reading on licensing |
| **eqlegendsdb.com** | ✅ | character profiles, build loadouts, live item data | robots allows all |
| **eqlegends.wiki** | ✅ | a second "EQL Wiki" — but its `robots.txt` points at `buildasoccersquadwiki.wiki` | treat with suspicion |

### gnollguard.com is off limits, and it is the one we would most want

Its `robots.txt` names AI agents explicitly and refuses them:

```
User-Agent: GPTBot
User-Agent: OAI-SearchBot
User-Agent: ChatGPT-User
User-Agent: ClaudeBot
User-Agent: Claude-Web
User-Agent: anthropic-ai
...
Disallow: /
```

The network egress proxy blocks it independently (`EGRESS_BLOCKED`). **I did not fetch
anything from it beyond `robots.txt`, and will not.** It is a crowd-sourced loot database
with drop locations — precisely the data that would close the gap in §6 with real
observations. If we want it, the route is a person asking its operator, not a crawler.

### On licensing

`eqldb.org` describes itself as using "EQL Wiki catalogue data adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0".
This project's own `meta.json` states the opposite about the upstream:

> *"eqlwiki publishes no content licence — checked 2026-08-18; siteinfo rightsinfo empty,
> Project:Copyrights absent."*

A third party's claim about someone else's licence is not authority, and I have **not**
re-checked eqlwiki's `rightsinfo` as part of this. If the wiki has since published a
licence that would be a material change to this project's attribution position and is
worth a deliberate check.

## 8. Spells: we do not model them at all

The shipped payload is `items-index.json`, `items/*.json`, `focus-effects.json`,
`contamination.json`, `meta.json`. **There is no spell data.** The app's only use of the
word is item spell-mods and focus effects, and `focus-effects.json` deliberately carries
"no numbers a planner could score".

The owner asked about **Call of Flame** for Rangers. The wiki has it, fully structured:

```
Ranger — Level 49 · 300 damage · 106 mana · 0.50s cast · 10.00s recast
Skill: Evocation · Resist: Fire (0) · from the Call of Flame Quest · {{Classic Era}}
```

`jmoyers-spells.json`, `jmoyers-class-spells.json` and `jmoyers-spelllines-merged.json`
are already vendored in `research/data/` and are read by nothing.

Adding spells is a **scope expansion**, not a gap fix — it is a second entity type, a
second ranking model, and a second payload. It should be a deliberate decision.

**And to answer the question directly: no other session has told me anything about spell
upgrades.** I have no working channel to sessions C, D or E — `ListAgents` reports no
reachable peers and cross-session messaging fails at the auth layer. Everything I know
about their work comes from the Director's `HANDOFF.md`.

## 9. What was actually shipped, and what it broke on the way in

Written after the work, against the built payload rather than against §5's estimate.

`pipeline/wiki-supplement.mjs` fetched **2,366 pages** from the live wiki — the 207 this
project had never seen, plus the era-less records §4 measured — and vendored them as
`research/data/eqlwiki-supplement-2026-09-02.json`, carrying the raw `statsblock` wikitext
unparsed so that `build.mjs` remains the only parser. Wired into the build as a fifth
source, always last in every pick list.

| | count |
|---|---|
| Catalogue before / after | **3,663 → 3,883** (+220) |
| Of the 220, wearable | 82 |
| Of the 220, wearable and carrying stats | 64 |
| Supplement pages on the wiki's `VerifiedPages` | 139 |
| Existing records changed | **0** |
| Records removed | **0** |

`node pipeline/build.mjs` produces the first four rows; the last two are its own
`REMOVED` / `EXISTING CHANGED` lines. §5 predicted 66; the true figure is 82 wearable,
because the era-less recheck landed better than the 28 measured in §4.

### The parser fix that made most of it work

The first run reported **14 unrecognised slot tokens** and race tokens reading `ALLBR`
and `NONEBR`. The live wiki writes its stats blocks with `<br>` separators where the
vendored scrape used newlines, so every field was being read with the tag glued to its
value. `parseStatsBlock` now strips `<br>` before splitting, and both counts fell to
`(none)`.

### The Tier M violation it introduced, and the guards that caught it

The supplement brought in **`Shadow Rage Tunic`** — a seventh piece of the one set the
player has confirmed, carrying a wiki `Classic` era and a full stat block. That is
precisely what the player's standing instruction forbids: *"If the stat block that you
have for it is from out of Era, do not include it until I tell you and supply you with
correct stats for every item."* The six pieces already in the catalog ship era-less and
unstatted for that reason; the seventh arrived a month later and walked straight past the
rule, because the rule was written as a list of six names.

It was caught by the test suite before it reached anything, and then again by
`verify.mjs`, which rejected the *first* fix for admitting an era-less item under no
stated rule. The item now ships held to its siblings' standing: no era, no stats, marked,
findable but never scoreable.

Re-damaging the built payload — giving the Tunic back its `AC: 23`, its three attributes
and its `Classic` era — fails **6 of the 1,176 unit tests** and one `verify.mjs` check.
That matters more than it looks: CI runs `tsc`, `vitest` and `npm run build` and **does
not run the pipeline**, so the guard that protects this in CI is the vitest one, and it
was confirmed to fail rather than assumed to. The payload was restored and re-checked
byte-identical by SHA-256 afterwards.

The case for trusting its numbers is genuinely better than for the others — its page
restricts the item to **BER**, and Berserker is not a class in the era of original
EverQuest this wiki is contaminated by, so it is poor evidence of a same-named
original-EQ item; it drops in the Planes of Fear and Hate, where the player placed the
set; and it is on `VerifiedPages`. **That is an argument, and the instruction asked for
verified numbers.** Restoring its stat block is a client capture away, and it is the
owner's call, not this session's.

It ships under a fourth purge reason, `piece of a confirmed set` (1 item), kept
deliberately separate from `player-confirmed` (6 items) so the ship-reason table never
claims the owner named something they did not. `verify.mjs` admits it by name only, and
only for as long as it carries nothing scoreable.

### Two pre-existing defects found while re-running the gate

Neither was caused by this work; both were found because the counts moved.

1. **The Sources page told every reader the payload disagreed with itself.** It compared
   the catalogue size against `purge.shipped` without the `admittedOutsideScrape` term
   the pipeline publishes *specifically so the two reconcile* — so on every build that
   admitted any existence-only record, which is every build, it printed *"The payload
   disagrees with itself; rebuild before trusting either."* The figures reconciled
   exactly the whole time: 3,873 + 10 = 3,883. This is the F07 shape again — a guard over
   the data would have passed throughout, because the data was never wrong. The new guard
   asserts the **rendered** text, and fails when the comparison is reverted.

2. **`meta.dataReliability.dmgBonus` carried a hand-typed census that was wrong.** It
   said *"no source carries it per item; jmoyers has it on 1 item only."* Measured:
   jmoyers carries a `dmgBonus` field on **0** of its 11,375 records and prints a
   `Dmg Bon` line in **4** stats blocks; the vendored August scrape prints it in **0**;
   the live wiki prints it in **4**. Three now reach the payload — `Cherista's Fangs`,
   `Cursed Blade`, `Rod of Understanding` — the first damage bonuses this catalogue has
   ever shipped. The note is now computed from the payload it describes.

   The fourth, `Skycleaver`, does not ship its bonus, and that is the merge policy rather
   than a loss: a weapon block is taken whole from the first source supplying damage or
   delay, so a field printed by a later source for an item an earlier one already
   described is not grafted on. Whether this game stores the bonus per item or derives it
   from level and delay is still unsettled, so nothing here was reconstructed.

# Addendum, 3 September 2026 — the era rule was wrong, and the wiki can partly say so

The owner corrected the premise this catalogue's era gate rests on:

> *"EQ Legends is built in the classic era, but they have brought certain things from
> future expansions into classic, so this cannot be a rule, rather a starting point.
> Start with EQ classic era, then verify."*

Froglok, Kerran and Iksar were added as races; Beastlord and Berserker as classes; items
and gear sets were added for them — Shadow Rage being one. So "the wiki calls it Velious"
never meant "this game does not have it", and the purge produces false negatives by
construction. Both vocabularies were already complete: `RACES` carries all 15 and
`CLASSES` all 16, so the app has always offered Iksar and Berserker.

## 10. The wiki answers the question directly — for one half of it

`eqlwiki.com` runs an `EQLClientData` extension exposing a POST-only
`action=eqlmetadata`, which returns per page:

```
outOfEra   the wiki's own determination FOR EVERQUEST LEGENDS
missing    whether the page exists
touched    last edit
eraRevision 156232 — the config it was computed from
```

That is the wiki making a statement about *this game*, rather than a statement about
original EverQuest content that we then reason over. `node pipeline/probe-wiki-era.mjs`
asks it about all 11,467 titles this project knows.

### The trap, and it caught me first

The first reading of that probe said **3,255 withheld items are in the game**, including
2,208 of the 2,213 in the largest quarantine bucket. That reading is wrong, and the
correction is the important part of this section.

`outOfEra: false` is the **default for a page the wiki has not tagged**. Measured:
`10 Dose Ant's Potion` is a player-crafted alchemy item with no era template, no drop
zone and no era signal of any kind, and it returns `false`. So `false` is the absence of
a mark, never evidence of presence. Acting on it would have admitted ~7,100 titles on the
strength of nobody having said anything about them — the same inference-as-confirmation
mistake that once put five FearHateRevamp sets in front of a player as "EQL planar gear".

`true` is different: it is a positive statement somebody made. `Tome of Miragul` carries
`{{Kunark Era}}` and returns `true`.

**So only the `true` verdicts are vendored and only they are used.**
`research/data/eqlwiki-era-verdicts-2026-09-03.json` holds 4,336 of them and nothing else.

### What it actually found

| | count |
|---|---|
| Titles asked about | 11,467 |
| Marked out of era by the wiki | 4,336 |
| Not marked (absence of a tag, not evidence) | 7,124 |
| No such page | 7 |
| **Items we were SHIPPING that the wiki marks out of era** | **7** |

The seven: `Bloodclaw Battle Axe`, `Bloodclaw Mace`, `Blue Crystal Staff`,
`Fanged Skull Stiletto`, `Frosty Cave Trout`, `Tome of Miragul`, `Withered Leather Tunic`.
Every one shipped on an era of `Classic` derived from a non-wiki field, and **none carries
any Tier M evidence** — nothing observed contradicts the wiki. They are now withheld.

The rule runs **last** in `shipDecision`, after every Tier M test and after our own era
ladder. Placed first it also swallowed 4,333 items the ladder already excluded and
replaced "Scars of Velious" with a generic reason on the withheld page a player reads when
they cannot find something. It is used only for what nothing else can do: catching items
our own ladder would have shipped.

### What this does NOT fix

The 2,213 era-less records are unchanged. The wiki has no opinion on them either — its
`false` for them is the same absence as for the potion. §6's decision is still the owner's,
and the new evidence only sharpens it: **shipping them marked `eraUnknown` would not
contradict the wiki, because the wiki has not spoken.**

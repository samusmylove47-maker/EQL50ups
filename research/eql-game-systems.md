# EverQuest Legends — Game Systems & Sourcing Research

Compiled 16 August 2026. Research base: ~25 web searches plus a full local read of the
`samusmylove47-maker/eql-source` GitHub repository — which is the actual source tree of
**eqlsource.com** (published via Netlify; also mirrored at eqlsource.netlify.app). Because that
repo was readable directly, everything attributed to "EQL Source" below is quoted from the site's
own pages and internal docs, not from search snippets.

**Confidence vocabulary used in this document**
- **Confirmed** — stated by two or more independent sources (or by an official/Tier-1 source).
- **Single-source** — one credible source only; plausible but unverified.
- **Low-confidence** — only found on SEO lookalike wikis (see §2.3) or mixed classic/Legends text.
- **Open/Unknown** — explicitly unresolved, often flagged as open by EQL Source itself.
- Where useful, claims also carry the EQL Source tier (T1–T5, M, C) defined in §1.2.

## 1. eqlsource.com and its sourcing criteria

### 1.1 What eqlsource.com is

EQL Source ("Accurate, sourced and kept current") is an unofficial, fan-run **reference site**
for EverQuest Legends — static HTML, no server, no accounts, regenerated by Python scripts and
deployed from GitHub to Netlify. Its self-description: *"survey plates for every revamped
dungeon, 3D raid encounter guides, and progression tools."* Its founding complaint: *"most
EverQuest Legends reference material is classic EverQuest text in a Legends-shaped hole"* — i.e.
the community wiki began as a Project 1999 wiki fork and much of what players read about Legends
is actually 1999-2001 EverQuest text.

Site contents (confirmed from the repo):
- **Dungeon surveys ("plates")** — 13 zones surveyed: Najena, Lair of the Splitpaw, Crushbone,
  Befallen, Blackburrow, Lower Guk, Nagafen's Lair, The Hole, The Warrens, Castle Mistmoore
  (10 with full verification), plus Plane of Fear, Plane of Hate, Kedge Keep (unverified).
  Population tables, named-mob rosters with spawn data, loot tied to drop source, coordinates
  re-derived from /loc records, navigation maps.
- **The Index** — searchable item/named-mob index: 451 items, 233 named mobs at time of reading.
- **Raid guides** — Plane of Sky island-by-island, Eye of Veeshan rendered in 3D (three.js).
- **Tools** — character sheet (trio/race-unlock/Plane-of-Sky tracker, URL-encoded state, no
  server), race unlock tracker, race & primary calculator, faction impact checker (measured from
  combat logs), planar gear targets, inventory reader, Plane of Sky tracker.
- **Learn pages** — "Is it still true?" (register of inherited classic advice and whether it
  survived into Legends), "What difficulty changes", "Motes", "How raid access works", "Deity and
  the level 11 lock", "Reading a floor plan", "Contamination" (a self-audit scanner for classic-
  text contamination in its own pages).
- **Public data** — versioned JSON datasets (`/data/`): `zones.v1.json`, `items.v1.json`,
  `sightings.v1.json` (parsed drop sightings), `sky.v1.json` (all 16 classes' Plane of Sky
  quests). Attribution-required; measured data freely usable. *"Nobody else in this community
  publishes any, so we do."*
- **Credits** — contributors: THE GAME IS (mote/upgrade guide), Cavepig (Plane of Sky keying),
  Skyfox (planar loot), Annalise/AnnaWulf (underwater weapons, non-paladin epic completion,
  per-difficulty lockouts), BarakDur (planar gear tool idea), Classic XP (Plane of Hate farming),
  BrutallStatic (voidling locations — also a YouTube/Twitch creator), Avenrae and Shara (the
  combat logs behind every measured figure).

Every claim on the site names its source and the date it was read; open gaps are published as
gaps ("an open entry is the normal state, not a failure").

### 1.2 The tier system (eqlsource.com/sources — "Sourcing standard")

Public five-tier ladder, quoted from `public/sources.html` (confirmed, primary text):

| Tier | Name | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** (strongest) | **Official patch notes** | "Dated, authoritative, and they override everything below them. Anything published after a wiki page's last edit supersedes that page." (Also includes producer's letters and dev statements in official channels; first-party game files — e.g. the map files the client installs — are treated as Tier 1.) |
| **Tier 2** | **Structured wiki data** | "Infoboxes, NPC tables, item tables, coordinate records on eqlwiki. Machine-shaped fields that somebody entered from the live game." Also: fresh pages authored by a named editor describing systems with no classic equivalent (e.g. eqlwiki `Personal_Instance` by editor Caliente) — beta-era documentation of live features qualifies. |
| **Tier 3** | **Named community guides** | "eqprogression.com, and maintained wiki user guides such as Alanna's Race Unlock Guide. Named authors, actively updated, dated. Reliable, but they are one person's reading of the game." |
| **Tier 4** | **Aggregators** | "EQL Build Forge, EQ Legends Tools. Useful for cross-checking a number against a second pair of eyes. Each carries a snapshot date; anything older than the last patch is treated as stale." Player-consensus claims are also carried at T4. |
| **Tier 5** (marked on sight) | **Wiki prose** | "Large parts are a Project 1999 import, sometimes word for word. It describes a single-class game at fixed difficulty. It is quoted only when marked as classic, never as Legends fact." SEO lookalike domains are rated "Tier 5 at best." |

Presentation rule: **Tiers 1 and 2 print plain; anything weaker carries its badge (T3/T4/T5)
wherever it appears.**

Two additional tiers exist in the project's internal standard (`docs/SOURCES.md`):

- **Tier M — measured combat logs** ("instrument data"). *"Tier M outranks every read source for
  what it directly measures, and generalises to nothing at all beyond its stated conditions."*
  Figures must publish their conditions (trio, level, zone, difficulty, date, sample size); "a
  drop seen once is 'seen once', never a drop rate."
- **Tier C — a first-hand report, not yet an instrument reading** (added 10 Aug 2026). A named,
  dated, unconfirmed player report. "Sits below M and above 3." Must publish who, when, what
  would confirm it, and an unconfirmed badge; "a Tier C claim never becomes fact by repetition."
  The site's own collaborator's unparsed play reports are Tier C, not M.

Related methodology (internal, same file):
- Provenance test: a wiki page whose oldest revision is by a `P99Wiki>` / `imported>` author is a
  Project 1999 import; its **prose is T5 even when its infobox is current T2**. *"A tier 5
  sentence inside a tier 2 container is the most dangerous object in this ecosystem."*
- Verification standard for zones (three gates): full page fetch + roster re-compare; edit
  history fetched (not just footer date — the "stale-revision trap"); every plotted coordinate
  lands within 120 units of walkable geometry extracted from the game's own mesh files.
- A "contamination" scanner runs over the site's own pages hunting classic-format signatures
  (percentage haste, charge counts, `SV X +N` blocks, NO DROP wording).
- Change handling: "green" single-source factual field changes may be applied directly; "red"
  (new prose/mechanics/conflicts) require written reasoning; everything merges via PR by a human.

### 1.3 Note on sibling/tooling sites with similar sourcing pages

`eqltools.com/sources` (a different site, see §2.2) publishes a comparable sourcing statement:
claims carry a "source seal" — developer statements (patch notes, Discord dev channel), client-
mined data, wiki (CC BY-SA 4.0, explicitly "not confirmed" because user-submitted and P99-forked),
and player consensus ("believed, not confirmed"). `eql.gamertan.com/sources` (EQL Helper)
documents CC BY-SA 4.0 reuse of EQL Wiki content and preserves "Wiki, P99, Lucy, client"
identifiers as namespaced external evidence.

### 2.3 SEO lookalike farm — do not trust (EQL Source: "Tier 5 at best ... skip them")

EQL Source's internal watchlist names these **lookalike domains**: "They carry no bylines, no
edit history and no citations, and they reference 'developer updates' without linking any":
`eqlegends.wiki`, `everquestlegends.wiki`, `everquestlegends-wiki.wiki`,
`everquest-legends-wiki.wiki`. The same content-mill pattern (generic "August 2026 Guide" pages,
no authors, mutually copied text) is visible in search results for
`everquestlegendswiki.wiki` and **`everquestlegends.online`** — note that everquestlegends.online
is one of these guide farms, *not* the community wiki (the community wiki is eqlwiki.com).
Claims found only on these domains are marked low-confidence in this document. They rank highly
in search and are the main vector for classic-EQ text being presented as Legends fact.

## 3. Game identity and business model

- **Confirmed (T1/press):** Standalone MMORPG by Daybreak Game Company + indie studio Game Jawn
  (with Darkpaw), announced at GDC on 24 March 2026; closed beta began 24 April 2026; launched
  **28 July 2026**, PC-only, download from everquestlegends.com; staged server rollouts.
  Reimagined **pre-Kunark classic EverQuest** (original art style/zones/music) with modern
  systems. PvE-only; instanced dungeons and raids ("to prevent kill-stealing and ganking");
  designed so **every piece of main content can be completed solo**, while remaining
  group-compatible.
- **Confirmed:** **$19.99** base purchase (includes first month), then **$9.99/month**
  subscription.
- **Confirmed (T1 Producer's Letter, 8 Jul 2026 + press):** Cash-shop currency **Iridium (IR)**:
  $4.99 = 500 IR up to $99.99 = 12,000 IR. Shop sells unlock tokens — **deity 500 IR (~$5),
  race 1,000 IR (~$10), primary class 1,500 IR (~$15)** — plus equipment slots, storage/inventory
  expansions. Convenience/unlock-focused; no gear sales reported.
- **Pre-orders opened 16 June 2026** (given in task brief; consistent with a 16 June patch-notes
  date — treat as confirmed-by-context).
- Post-launch roadmap: a launch roadmap was shared at release (RPG Site); an expansions/Kunark
  discussion exists only on low-confidence sites. **Unknown.**

## 4. Core systems

### 4.1 Classes — confirmed list of 16

Confirmed by EQL Source's `sky.v1.json` (all 16 class codes) + multiple databases:

Warrior, Paladin, Shadow Knight, Ranger, Monk, Rogue, Berserker, Beastlord, Bard,
Cleric, Druid, Shaman, Enchanter, Magician, Necromancer, Wizard.

(All 14 classic-era classes plus Berserker and Beastlord, which were post-Kunark additions in
1999-2003 EverQuest — here available at launch. Role groupings commonly cited: tanks
WAR/PAL/SHD; healers CLR/DRU/SHM; melee DPS MNK/ROG/RNG/BER/BST; casters WIZ/MAG/NEC/ENC;
hybrid/support BRD.)

### 4.2 Races — confirmed list of 15

All 12 classic launch races — Human, Barbarian, Erudite, Wood Elf, High Elf, Dark Elf, Half Elf,
Dwarf, Halfling, Gnome, Ogre, Troll — plus **Iksar**, **Froglok**, and the new cat-folk
**Kerran** (Kerra Isle redesigned as their home; covered by Variety). 15 total (confirmed by
press and EQL Source's race tooling; one EQL Source page says "16 races" — likely counting an
extra grouping; treat 15 as correct).

- Starting cities follow classic geography (Qeynos, Freeport, Erudin, Kelethin, Felwithe,
  Kaladim, Ak'Anon, Grobb, Oggok, Halas, Rivervale, Neriak…); Kerran start on Kerra Isle; Iksar
  have a new original staging hub (single-source); Froglok start Gukta/Oggok-adjacent
  (single-source).
- **Race unlocks (confirmed, T2/T3):** races outside your initial set are unlockable in-game via
  long faction grinds — the definitive guide is **Alanna's Race Unlock Guide** on eqlwiki (EQL
  Source's race tools are built from it) — or bypassed with a store Race Unlock Token. Example
  from EQL Source's measured data: Erudite unlock requires Heretics/Deepwater Knights faction
  work (kill 400 kobolds, Kobold Molars turn-ins, killing Paineel heretics).
- Racials survive in some form (low-confidence details: Ogre frontal-stun immunity, Iksar/Troll
  regen + Iksar AC bonus, Troll Slam — plausible classic carryovers, SEO-sourced).
- **Legends-specific race/class matrix changes (T2, eqlwiki Character_Races rev. 163936):**
  e.g. **Kerran can take Druid; Wood Elf cannot take Beastlord.** Class availability per race was
  revised at launch.

### 4.3 Multiclassing (the defining system)

- **Confirmed:** every character runs **three classes at once** ("the trio"). Two chosen at
  creation — the **primary is race-restricted**, the second free — and a **third unlocks at
  level 10**. **560** distinct class trios (= C(16,3)) × 15 races is the commonly cited build
  space.
- **Confirmed (T1-adjacent):** at **level 11 the primary class, race, and deity lock
  permanently**; the only reversals are the store tokens (§3). Secondary and tertiary classes
  swap freely, but **only in cities/major towns** (one source: starting cities, city-adjacent
  newbie zones, and the Arena).
- **Confirmed:** your **effective level is the lowest of the three class levels**; swapping in a
  lower-leveled class drops you to its level. From 1-10 all classes gain XP simultaneously
  (duplicated, not split), so everyone reaches 10 with all classes at 10 (single-source detail).
- **Level 50 perk (T2, eqlwiki Newbie Guide):** reaching 50 grants a **free primary-class
  unlock**, and an unlocked primary becomes selectable **by any race**. Community guides also
  describe an earnable token route for swapping primary at 50.
- **Stat/pool combination (T4, eqltools.com "The trio system"):** each class computes its own HP
  and mana from its own formula, and the character gets **the sum of the best two**; **caps take
  the highest** of the three classes; **spell/ability access runs at the lowest** of the three
  levels. Single-source (aggregator) — the most detailed mechanical claim not yet corroborated.
- **Loadouts (confirmed, dev video Apr 2026 via MassivelyOP/MMOHuts):** saved presets bundling
  class combination, gear, hotbars and bandolier; each loadout stores its layout independently;
  swapping restricted as above.
- No XP penalties by race/class have been reported anywhere (classic hybrid penalties appear to
  be gone — inference from silence, unconfirmed).

### 4.4 Stats, resists, combat

- Attributes: classic seven — STR, STA, AGI, DEX, WIS, INT, CHA — appear throughout item data
  and guides (confirmed as present; exact formulas unknown). Item stat blocks carry classic
  fields: AC, HP, MANA, ATK implied, focus effects (e.g. "Burning Affliction II", "Extended
  Enhancement II", "Reanimation Efficiency I"), proc effects with level gates, clickables.
- **Resists:** classic five (Magic, Fire, Cold, Disease, Poison) **plus a new "SV Void"** —
  present on 5 items in EQL Source's mined catalogue (T2) and shown in fan character profiles.
  Void-themed systems (voidlings, Void Gems, "Void-touched Potential") are a Legends addition.
- **Haste — OPEN:** EQL Tools says Legends slow/haste are **flat values on an attack-speed
  stat** (100 base + 60 haste = 160, additive); eqlwiki's Haste Guide still carries the classic
  percentage formula. EQL Source treats this as unresolved and flags its own percentage tooltips
  as suspect. One screenshot of a haste tooltip would settle it.
- **Buffs (T2, eqlwiki Buff Lines):** buff slots run **1–8 plus a "Layer 2"** — a departure from
  classic's 15 slots. Stacking rules (e.g. Paragon of Spirit vs Clarity vs bard regen) are
  **open**; the wiki page does not cover many spells.
- **Stances & Invocations (confirmed, RPG Site SGF preview + guides):** new combat posture
  systems (absent from 1999 EQ). **Stances** (melee): Defensive / Offensive / Mage Hunter.
  **Invocations** (casters): e.g. Recover (mana regen), Over Channel (resist shred), Spell Blade
  (melee-proc a chosen spell). A stance and an invocation can run simultaneously on a hybrid
  trio. Specific numbers (e.g. "Offense doubles melee damage") are SEO-sourced — low confidence.
- Combat log: the game writes classic-style `/log` files (basis of the parser ecosystem);
  `ATTN Claude:`-style party-chat notes even get used as annotations by EQL Source. Zone-entry
  lines print instance difficulty (see §4.7).

### 4.5 Deities and factions

- **Deity chosen at creation; locks at level 11** with race/primary; reversible only via the
  500 IR token. Effects: starting faction standings, deity-gated items/quests, class
  availability interactions. (T1 lock/pricing; T2 standings.)
- eqlwiki's Starting Faction Standings table (T2, "requires cleanup" banner) covers **10 deities
  plus Agnostic across 19 starting cities**: Bertoxxulous, Bristlebane, Cazic Thule, Innoruuk,
  Prexus, Quellious, Rallos Zek, Solusek Ro, The Tribunal, Tunare. Fan database eqlbase.com lists
  the **full classic 16-pantheon** (adding Brell Serilis, Erollisi Marr, Karana, Mithaniel Marr,
  Rodcet Nife, Veeshan) — whether all 16 are selectable, or only a subset per race/class as in
  classic, is **unresolved** (the eqlwiki Deity page itself is a redirect stub).
- **Agnostic** minimizes faction friction at the cost of deity-gated items/perks; "always a safe
  bet" per the wiki's Newbie Guide (T3). How much content is deity-gated is **unpublished**;
  whether a deity token re-runs starting-faction adjustments is **unknown**.
- **Factions:** classic-style kill/quest faction system confirmed and measured — EQL Source's
  faction tool tracks **80 factions**, with per-kill hits measured from logs across 24 zones
  (e.g. Befallen: Burning Dead −2/kill; Mistmoore: −5 Agents of Mistmoore / +5 King Tearis Thex;
  Permafrost group: Lady Vox kill −409 Vox faction). Faction feeds race unlocks (§4.2).

### 4.6 Leveling, XP, AAs

- **Level cap 50** (confirmed across many sources).
- **ZEM (zone experience modifier) exists** and is published per zone by eqlwiki (EQL Source
  quotes ZEM 86–139 across its 13 zones, e.g. The Hole/Warrens 171%-equivalent vs Lower Guk
  115%). Con-color kill XP as in classic.
- **AAs (Alternate Advancement) — confirmed to exist at 50**; details largely from T3/T4
  sources: post-50 XP redirects into AA points; **a single AA pool shared across the trio's
  three classes**; categories General / Archetype / Class / Special (+ Tradeskill AAs, e.g.
  Crafting Mastery); no AA slider; AA rate driven by con color (~1/5 of leveling-speed at cap
  per one guide). eqltools.com ships an AA Planner (so the system is real); specific category
  names and rates are single-source.
- **Rituals (new system, T2/T3):** leveling specific classes past thresholds unlocks permanent
  "rituals" usable **regardless of which classes are currently active** — most famously
  druid/wizard self-ports. The exact unlock levels are **open** — eqlwiki's Rituals page and
  Travel Guide disagree (25/27 vs 19/29; EQL Source refuses to quote one; a popular guide says
  Druid 25 / Wizard 23).
- **Epic quests exist** (one per class, classic-style). Measured finding (Tier C, credited to
  Annalise): the **Fiery Avenger quest is not class-locked** — a non-paladin completed every
  step — but the reward item keeps `Class: PAL`. Whether this generalizes to other epics is
  explicitly untested.
- Skills/tradeskills: a classic-style use-to-raise skill system and the nine classic tradeskills
  are described by eqlwiki/EQProgression Legends pages (Blacksmithing, Tailoring, Jewelcrafting,
  Fletching, Alchemy, Tinkering, Pottery, Baking, Brewing; ~200 cap with an AA to break it) —
  **mixed classic/Legends sourcing, low-to-medium confidence.**

### 4.7 Difficulty scaling (D0–D4) — a signature system

Confirmed via EQL Source Tier-M logs + EQL Tools measurements (two independent lines):

- Five tiers with in-game names printed on the zone line: **D0 Base/Normal** (open world),
  **D1 Awakened**, **D2 Adaptive**, **D3 Fused**, **D4 Refined** (current max). Example log:
  `You have entered Befallen 1 (Awakened).`
- **Difficulty does not raise mob levels** (con colors identical at every tier). It scales HP,
  damage, resists, AC, mana, movement speed, aggro radius (~linearly per tier: HP ×1.15/×1.30 at
  D1/D2 multiplayer, ×1.10/×1.20 solo; D3-D4 multipliers **unpinned**), and gives mobs
  **player-style class kits** — published from D2, but EQL Source's logs caught trash mobs
  backstabbing + casting at **D1** in Mistmoore (Tier M).
- XP bonus per tier ×1.15/×1.30/×1.45/×1.60 multiplayer (×1.10-1.40 solo) — measured by EQL
  Tools but "whether the bonus is currently live" is flagged unpinned.
- **Loot: same table, pre-upgraded condition.** Items drop at `+N`; the difficulty is a floor
  (in 2,331 logged drops none fell below the zone's tier; ~12% rolled above). A bare item is
  the +0 form.
- Loot lockouts track **per difficulty** (player-reported, Tier C), so the same boss is worth
  running at each tier.
- SEO-wiki claims of per-player difficulty selection within one group ("D1 and D3 players in the
  same group each get their own scaling/loot") are **low-confidence** — not corroborated by any
  real source found.

### 4.8 Zones and world

- **Confirmed:** launch world is classic pre-Kunark Norrath — continents **Antonica, Faydwer,
  Odus** (plus the ocean zones between) with original-style geography and cities, plus new/
  redesigned areas: Kerra Isle (Kerran start), a new Iksar staging hub, revamped dungeons.
- **Zone count: uncertain.** A fan zone-connection-graph repo (`lab1702/eql-maps`) counts
  **77 launch zones**; the game client ships **194 map files** (includes sub-labels; T1 but not
  a zone count); SEO sites variously say 16 / 73 / 114 — unreliable. Best estimate: ~75-80
  zones, matching classic pre-Kunark plus additions.
- **Legends renames some zones** (from log lines, Tier M): Upper Guk → "The City of Guk",
  Lower Guk → "The Ruins of Old Guk", The Hole → "The Ruins of Old Paineel", plus "The Castle of
  Mistmoore", "The Permafrost Caverns", "Temple of Cazic-Thule", etc.
- **Dungeon revamp program (T1 patch notes):** Blackburrow (2 Jun), Najena (23 Jun — "a striking
  lack of placeholders for named mobs"), Crushbone + Splitpaw (14 Jul), launch-day pass (28 Jul:
  The Hole, Nagafen's Lair, Lower Guk, Splitpaw, The Warrens, Castle Mistmoore, Upper Guk,
  Crushbone, Befallen, Blackburrow, Najena — "placeholders removed and maximum respawn times
  lowered"; "more dungeon treatments will take place over time"). **Named-mob placeholders are
  gone in all 11 revamped dungeons** — named spawn every cycle (T1, corroborated in play).
  Revamps add new named mobs and entirely new loot (e.g. Crushbone's six new named; Blackburrow's
  Splitpaw-refugee cast).
- Sample level bands (T1/T2): Crushbone 4-22, Blackburrow 5-20, Warrens 4-25, Befallen 7-25+,
  Najena 8-35 (tuned 15-30), Splitpaw 25-28 entrance → 40-42 deep, Mistmoore 20-45, Lower Guk
  24-49, Kedge Keep 32-50, Nagafen's Lair 25-55, The Hole 39-56, Plane of Fear/Hate 46+.
- Respawn timers were rebalanced (e.g. Lower Guk zone respawn 9:28, was 28:00 classic); current
  post-28-July values unpublished (T1 says "lowered" without figures).

### 4.9 Instances, dungeons, raids

Three instance types (T1 patch notes + T2 eqlwiki Personal_Instance — EQL Source's
"How raid access works"):

1. **Public** — shared zone instance; enter by zone line; no charges; no raid bosses.
2. **Personal** — your own copy of a zone for grouping/farming; entered at zone level;
   **2 charges, regain one per hour**; no raid bosses.
3. **Raid** — created by hailing NPCs called **voidlings**; lockout-timed; the only place raid
   bosses exist.

- **Open-world raid bosses no longer spawn** (T1, 16 Jun 2026 patch: Maestro of Rancor,
  Innoruuk, Cazic Thule, Fright, Terror, Dread, a dracoliche named individually).
- **Group cap: 4 players. Raid cap: 8 players.** (Stated plainly by EQL Source from its source
  base; PLANES.md: "Legends caps a raid at 8.")
- **Solo raid versions exist** (T1: "Balance changes to Solo Raid versions of Innoruuk,
  Cazic-Thule, Maestro of Rancor, and Lord of Loathing"). Difficulty D0-D4 selectable at raid
  creation.
- **Loot lockouts (T1):** weekly bonus-loot lockout resets **Tuesdays 8 AM PST**; a kill while
  locked still yields **one guaranteed drop** from the boss's unique table (28 Jul patch).
  Per-difficulty lockout tracking is Tier C (player-reported). Whether solo and group lockouts
  are shared or separate is **open** (a 6 May dev stream said decoupled; community summaries say
  shared weekly).
- **Raid roster (launch endgame):** dragons **Lord Nagafen** (Nagafen's Lair) and **Lady Vox**
  (Permafrost; ~level 55, ~32k base HP per wiki data), planar gods **Cazic-Thule** (Plane of
  Fear, via the Feerrott) and **Innoruuk** (Plane of Hate — entered from the **Oasis of Marr**,
  no wizard port needed), sub-bosses (Maestro of Rancor, Fright/Terror/Dread, a dracoliche /
  Lord of Loathing), and the **Plane of Sky** gauntlet. Boss stat corpus on the wiki is largely
  classic import (T5) except Innoruuk's Legends-updated HP: **156,000 (D2 Raid) / 235,000 (D4
  Raid)** (T2).
- **Plane of Sky (confirmed structure, EQL Source sky.v1.json):** floating-island ladder, each
  island's guardian drops the key upward: Island 1 Fairy (Thunder Spirit Princess) → 1.5 Noble
  (Noble Dojorn, weekly) → 2 Imp (Protector of Sky) → 3 Harpy (Gorgalosk) → 4 Pegasus (Keeper of
  Souls) → 5 Bird (Spiroc Lord, farmable) → 6 Bee (Bazzt Zzzt) → 7 Drake (Sister of the Spire,
  farmable) → 8 Butterfly (**Eye of Veeshan**, the capstone). Per-class quest lines with
  turn-ins per island (all 16 classes; 5 classes' reward stat blocks still unconfirmed).
  Supports the 8-player raid format and solo-capable instances.
- Pre-launch note: a 6 May 2026 dev stream "blew up" the original raid system design (GeeksVsGeeks
  coverage) — raiding was reworked into the voidling/instance model before launch.

### 4.10 Itemization and upgrade economy

- **Item upgrade tiers +0 → +10 (confirmed):** raise an item by **merging** another copy of the
  same item into it or feeding it **Motes of Potential**. Tier thresholds double (1, 2, 4 …
  512 units to T10).
- **Motes of Potential — 10 ranks** (T3, from the creator infographic shared by devs):
  Infinitesimal, Minor, Lesser, (plain) Potential, Major, Greater, Superior, Grand, Ascendant,
  Infinite. A mote is worth **2^(rank−1) spell XP** but only **rank item XP** ("spells scale
  doubled, items scale flat"). You may only apply a mote equal to the item's current tier or one
  rank above. Two motes convert up to one of the next rank — free for spell value, always a loss
  for item value. Conversion construct accepts ranks 1-8 only per the 21 Jun note (conflict with
  10-rank table — open).
- **Spell upgrades (confirmed):** motes invested directly into scribed spells (right-click-hold
  a spell gem) raise spell tiers — currently improving cast time, resist, duration; damage/heal
  scaling "on the way" (bard AoE songs already scale damage since a 14 July patch).
- **Void Gems / "Void-touched Potential" (T1+T3):** raid reward earned up to 3×/week via
  voidlings; each advances an item a full rank; community guidance: motes through +7, gems for
  +8-+10. (Whether the two names are the same object is unconfirmed.)
- **Exaltations (confirmed, MassivelyOP preview 27 Apr 2026):** redesign of classic augments —
  merge the focus/proc/click/worn effects and ornamentation appearance of one item into another;
  slots unlock with item level: Ornamentation, Focus, Click, Worn, Proc. A separate
  **Exaltation-point** account-wide passive system (health/mana/regen/proficiencies) is
  described by one T3 source (single-source).
- Focus effects, procs, clickables, attunable gear, ornamentation and No-Trade/Lore flags all
  exist (T2 item records); planar armor: the classic per-class-group split is gone — haunted
  chests and phoboplasms drop **two shared planar sets** (credited finding, Tier C→T2).
- "Advanced Loot Menu", find command, bandolier, macros — QoL layers confirmed in guide videos.
